# Reset Your Password

If you forgot your TaskFlow password, you can reset it from the sign-in screen.

## Reset by email

1. Open the TaskFlow sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Enter the email address associated with your account.
4. Open the password reset email from TaskFlow.
5. Follow the link and create a new password.

Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.

## If the email does not arrive

Check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. You can request another reset email after 60 seconds.

If you no longer have access to the email address on the account, contact TaskFlow Support for account recovery.
