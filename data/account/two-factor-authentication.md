# Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds an additional verification step when you sign in to TaskFlow.

## Enable 2FA

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Security**.
3. Choose **Two-factor authentication**.
4. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app.
5. Enter the six-digit verification code.

TaskFlow provides recovery codes when 2FA is enabled. Store these codes somewhere secure.

## Lost authenticator access

Use one of your recovery codes to sign in. If you cannot access your authenticator or recovery codes, contact TaskFlow Support.
