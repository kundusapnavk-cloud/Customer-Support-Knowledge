# Change Your Account Email

You can change the email address used to sign in to TaskFlow from your account settings.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Account**.
3. Choose **Email address**.
4. Enter your new email address.
5. Confirm the change using the verification email sent to the new address.

Your current password may be required before the change is accepted.

## Important

The new email address cannot already belong to another TaskFlow account.

If your organization uses single sign-on, your email address may be controlled by your workspace administrator.
