# Delete Your TaskFlow Account

You can permanently delete your TaskFlow account from account settings.

## Before deleting

Deleting your account removes your personal profile and access to all workspaces that depend on that account.

If you own a workspace, transfer ownership before deleting your account.

## Delete the account

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Account**.
3. Choose **Delete account**.
4. Enter your password.
5. Confirm permanent deletion.

Account deletion begins immediately. Some backup copies may remain temporarily according to TaskFlow's data retention policy.
