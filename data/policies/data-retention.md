# Data Retention

TaskFlow retains active account and workspace data while the service is being used.

When an account or workspace is deleted, TaskFlow begins deletion of associated customer data.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, security logs, billing records, or other systems where retention is required for operational or legal reasons.

Backup copies are removed according to TaskFlow's internal retention schedule and are not intended to remain available as active workspace data.
