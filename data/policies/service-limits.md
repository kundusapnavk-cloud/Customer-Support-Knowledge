# TaskFlow Service Limits

TaskFlow applies service limits to protect reliability and prevent abuse.

Limits may apply to storage, file uploads, automation runs, integrations, API requests, workspace membership, and other features.

The exact limit may depend on the subscription plan.

If a user reaches a limit, TaskFlow may display a warning or prevent additional activity until usage decreases or the workspace is upgraded.

Business customers can contact TaskFlow Support for questions about higher-capacity requirements.
