# Privacy Overview

TaskFlow processes account and workspace information to provide the service.

This may include account identifiers, workspace content, usage information, and technical information required for security and reliability.

TaskFlow does not require workspace owners to make private project content public.

Access to workspace information is controlled by account authentication and workspace permissions.

For legal or regulatory privacy requests, users should contact TaskFlow Support through the official privacy request channel.
