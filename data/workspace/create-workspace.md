# Create a Workspace

Workspaces let teams organize projects, members, and shared settings.

## Create a workspace

1. Open the workspace switcher.
2. Select **Create workspace**.
3. Enter a workspace name.
4. Choose a workspace URL.
5. Select **Create**.

The person who creates the workspace becomes its initial owner.

You can invite members after the workspace is created.
