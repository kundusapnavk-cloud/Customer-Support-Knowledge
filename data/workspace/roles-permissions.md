# Workspace Roles and Permissions

TaskFlow uses workspace roles to control access.

## Owner

Owners can manage billing, workspace settings, members, and ownership.

## Admin

Admins can manage members and workspace configuration but may have limited billing access.

## Member

Members can access projects and features available to them based on project permissions.

## Guest

Guests have restricted access to explicitly shared projects.

Some advanced permission controls are available only on the Business plan.
