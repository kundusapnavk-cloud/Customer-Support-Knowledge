# Invite Members to a Workspace

Workspace owners and permitted administrators can invite new members.

## Invite by email

1. Open **Workspace settings**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Choose **Invite members**.
4. Enter one or more email addresses.
5. Select the role for the new members.
6. Send the invitation.

Invitations remain pending until the recipient accepts them.

Expired invitations can be resent from the Members page.
