# Remove a Workspace Member

Workspace owners and permitted administrators can remove members.

## Remove a member

1. Open **Workspace settings**.
2. Select **Members**.
3. Find the member.
4. Open the member actions menu.
5. Select **Remove from workspace**.

Removing a member immediately removes their workspace access.

Tasks and comments created by the removed member remain in the workspace unless deleted separately.
