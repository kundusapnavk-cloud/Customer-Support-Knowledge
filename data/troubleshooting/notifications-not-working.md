# Notifications Are Not Working

If TaskFlow notifications are missing, check both TaskFlow preferences and your device settings.

## Check TaskFlow settings

Open **Settings > Notifications** and confirm that the notification type is enabled.

## Check device permissions

Make sure your browser or mobile operating system allows notifications from TaskFlow.

## Email notifications

Check your spam folder and confirm that your account email address is correct.

Changes to notification settings may take a short time to apply.
