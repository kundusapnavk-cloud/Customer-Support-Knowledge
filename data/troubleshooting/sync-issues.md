# Troubleshoot Sync Issues

TaskFlow normally synchronizes changes automatically.

If updates are not appearing:

1. Confirm that your internet connection is active.
2. Refresh the page or reopen the mobile app.
3. Sign out and sign back in.
4. Check whether the issue affects one project or the entire workspace.

For connected services such as Google Calendar, synchronization may take several minutes.

If the problem persists, contact TaskFlow Support with the affected project and approximate time of the issue.
