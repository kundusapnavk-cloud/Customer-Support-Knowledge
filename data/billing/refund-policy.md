# Refund Policy

TaskFlow subscription charges are generally non-refundable once a billing period has started.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged more than once or charged incorrectly, contact TaskFlow Support with the workspace name, invoice number, and transaction date.

## Subscription cancellation

Cancelling a subscription prevents future renewals but does not automatically refund the current billing period.

Refund exceptions may be considered where required by applicable law or when TaskFlow confirms a billing error.
