# View and Download Invoices

Workspace owners and billing administrators can view subscription invoices.

## Download an invoice

1. Open the workspace.
2. Go to **Settings**.
3. Select **Billing**.
4. Open **Invoices**.
5. Select the invoice you want to download.

Invoices include the billing period, subscription amount, taxes when applicable, and payment status.

Only users with billing permissions can access workspace invoices.
