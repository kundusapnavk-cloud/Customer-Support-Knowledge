# Cancel a TaskFlow Subscription

Workspace owners can cancel a Pro or Business subscription from billing settings.

## Cancel your subscription

1. Open **Workspace settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Choose **Manage subscription**.
4. Select **Cancel subscription**.
5. Confirm the cancellation.

Your paid subscription remains active until the end of the current billing period.

After the billing period ends, the workspace is downgraded to the Free plan unless otherwise stated during cancellation.
