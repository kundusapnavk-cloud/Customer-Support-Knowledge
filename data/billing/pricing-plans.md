# TaskFlow Pricing Plans

TaskFlow offers Free, Pro, and Business plans.

## Free

The Free plan includes core task management, personal projects, and limited workspace collaboration.

## Pro

The Pro plan includes advanced project views, additional integrations, automation rules, and increased storage.

## Business

The Business plan includes centralized administration, advanced permissions, team management controls, and priority support.

Subscription pricing may vary by billing cycle and region. The amount shown during checkout is the amount that applies to your subscription.
