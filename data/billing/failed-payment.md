# Failed Subscription Payments

A subscription payment may fail because of an expired card, insufficient funds, bank restrictions, or incorrect billing information.

## What happens after a failed payment

TaskFlow may retry the payment automatically.

During the retry period, your paid features may remain available for a limited time.

## Fix the payment

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Billing**.
3. Choose **Payment method**.
4. Update your payment details.
5. Retry the payment if the option is available.

If the payment continues to fail, contact your bank or payment provider.
