# Google Calendar Integration

TaskFlow can sync task due dates with Google Calendar.

## Connect Google Calendar

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Integrations**.
3. Choose **Google Calendar**.
4. Select **Connect**.
5. Sign in to your Google account.
6. Grant the requested permissions.

TaskFlow creates calendar events for supported task due dates.

Changes may take several minutes to appear after synchronization.
